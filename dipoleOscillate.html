The physics

τ = p × E   ·   U = −p · E

The dipole moment p points from −q to +q. The uniform field gives zero net force, but generally a nonzero torque about the center.

Stable: p aligned with E. Unstable: p opposite E. At exactly opposite alignment, the torque is zero; a small disturbance starts rotation.

Rigid permanent dipole with fixed center and fixed p. Time and field strength use illustrative units.

Angle versus time

Orientation from the rightward horizontal, wrapped to ±180°. Jumps at ±180° represent continuous rotation.
