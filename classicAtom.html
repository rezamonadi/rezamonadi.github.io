Why does the proton move?

The external field pushes the proton along E. The negative cloud attracts it back toward the cloud’s center.

eE = e²d / (4πε₀R³)

At equilibrium, the two forces balance. The cloud stays fixed as our reference; d is the relative separation.

A bigger cloud is easier to polarize
